ABSTRACT  
The current network security defense decision method is based on the principle of attack – defense confrontation 
and generally uses a random network model to project the real network. Most of the existing complex network 
defense decision methods consider a single defender while ignoring the confrontation and dependence between 
network attack and defense behaviors. In addition, the pertinence and dynamics of the defense effect are also 
insufficient. This article comprehensively considers the structure of a scale-free network and characteristics of 
network attack and defense behavior and combines a complex network with differential game theory to study the 
real-time defense decision problem. First, a propagation model based on a scale-free network is designed to 
realize the real-time fluctuation analysis of a network security state. Then, the attack and defense strategy are 
described, and the calculation method of revenue based on the confrontation analysis of the attack and defense 
behavior and a change in the security state of the network nodes based on the saddle point equilibrium strategy 
are introduced. Finally, based on the average connection degree of the real word Internet, a simulated network is 
constructed to conduct experiments. By comparison with random defense strategies and several typical complex 
network defense decision methods, the effectiveness and advancement of the proposed method are verified.   
1. Introduction 
Network information system has become a key infrastructure that 
society relies on for production and living. At the same time, network 
security problems have been emerging in an endless stream, and due to 
the increasing impact of network attacks, the consequences of losses 
have become more severe. In 2019, the Venezuelan power system was 
subjected to a cyberattack, which paralyzed the power grid and seriously 
affected people ’ s daily life. In 2020, NASA contractors were attacked by 
DoppelPaymer ransomware, and 2583 servers were hijacked, resulting 
in a large amount of data leakage [1] . Further, in 2021, in the United 
States, the software supplier Kaseya was accused by REvil for of security 
flaws and was forced to pay a large amount of indemnity. In 2021, the 
fuel pipeline transportation system of Colonial Company in the United 
States was attacked, and the oil pipeline was forced to stop, causing 
massive losses [ 2 , 3 ]. The safe operation of networks is related to the 
normal operation of social life. Therefore, how to effectively defend 
against network attacks and improve network security and stability has 
become a top priority. Due to complex, endless, and constantly updated 
network attacks, how to implement safe, effective, scientific, and 
reasonable network defense has become a common problem. 
Considering the concealment and diversity of network attacks, the 
vulnerability of the network environment itself, and inevitable vulner -
abilities of hardware devices, operating systems, application software, 
and network protocols [4] , network security defenders are relatively 
passive in the attack – defense confrontation. An ideal network defense 
strategy can reinforce all network weaknesses to defend against all 
possible network attacks. However, in the actual network 
attack – defense confrontation, a defender is constrained by various 
conditions, such as resources, capabilities, time, and cost. It is necessary 
to allocate defense resources scientifically and efficiently and select 
defense strategies reasonably to achieve a balance between security, 
performance, and cost. In addition to ensuring the balance, achieving 
realistic and optimal network security defense goals is also necessary [ 5 ,

Game theory is a mathematical method to study decision problems 
considering their conflicts and confrontations, which can provide 
theoretical tools for solving the network defense decision problem [7]. 
Recent research has achieved a series of results [8–11]. According to the 
dynamic analysis ability and real-time nature of the game model and 
decision algorithm, the current network defense decision methods based 
on the game theory can be divided into two categories: multi-stage game 
and real-time game. The multi-stage game divides the process of game 
theory into multiple stages according to the decision behavior of par -
ticipants. In the process of network attack and defense, the behavior of 
both the attacker and the defender generally has sequential order. 
Therefore, the dynamic stage game theory model can be used to model 
the attack and defense behavior. In [12], the signal game method was 
used to build a multi-stage moving target defense game theory model, 
which considers the signal attenuation in the game process and designs 
an active defense strategy on this basis. In [13], a moving target defense 
method based on the signal game was proposed for a type of DDoS 
attack, the Stealthy Link Flooding Attack (SLFA), and an optimal defense 
strategy was developed by calculating the Nash equilibrium. This 
strategy can effectively mitigate the SLFA attacks. Reference [14] 
combined stochastic game and reinforcement learning to make the de -
fender role gradually approach the optimal strategy through online 
learning. In [15], a multi-stage evolutionary game was used to analyze 
the network attack and defense behavior, and a reward learning 
mechanism was proposed to calculate an optimal defense strategy based 
on solving the Nash equilibrium. 
Due to the poor real-time analysis ability of discrete multi-stage 
game models, there is a lag in the characterization of attack and de -
fense behaviors and results, and it is difficult to model and describe 
continuous changes in the network security status, which cannot meet 
the requirements for the real-time decision of network defense. There -
fore, a game theory model that can continuously model and analyze 
network attack and defense behaviors in real-time and provide a basis 
for the decision is needed. A differential game is a theoretical tool to 
study the adversarial behavior and decision methods of participants in 
continuous real-time situations [16], which meets the requirements for 
the real-time analysis and real-time decision of network attack and de -
fense. Reference [17] aimed at the spread of malicious programs in 
wireless sensor networks and used differential games to study how to 
select an optimal method to suppress the spread of malicious programs 
under the constraint of minimal energy consumption. In [18], IoT de -
vices were used as network resources, a differential game model of the 
attack and defense behavior in an IoT environment was constructed, and 
an optimal defense effect was obtained based on solving the saddle point 
equilibrium. Reference [19] studied the DDoS defense decision problem 
and used differential games to model an interactive behavior of attack 
and defense to reduce the risk of DDoS at the lowest defense cost. In 
[20], the authors considered the random factors in the network attack 
and defense process and proposed a Markov differential game model to 
analyze the attack and defense behavior. Combined with the moving 
target defense method, an optimal defense strategy selection algorithm 
was designed. Reference [21] used differential game theory to model 
attack and defense behavior and considered the strategy behavior using 
the black- and white-box models of attackers and defenders, which 
improved the performance and effectiveness of defense strategies. 
However, the aforementioned articles have a common deficiency, 
namely, that a random network model is used for modeling research of a 
target network. These studies assumed that all nodes in a network are 
homogeneous nodes, and that the connection degree distribution of 
network nodes is approximately Poisson distribution. Attack and de -
fense behavioral game analysis, defensive decision algorithm design, 
and experimental verification were all based on these assumptions. 
However, the basic assumptions of random networks are not in accor -
dance with the actual situation. The Internet, World Wide Web, power 
grid, and large local area networks in the real world are not actually 
random but complex networks with scale-free properties [22,23]. Due to 
the underlying assumptions of network type conflicts with reality, the 
practical significance of the aforementioned research results is weak -
ened. For a complex network like the Internet, the degree of network 
nodes obeys a power-law distribution, which shows high heterogeneity. 
In such a network, very few central nodes have many connections, while 
most nodes have a small number of connections. Also, these networks 
are robust to random attacks but vulnerable to deliberate attacks [24]. 
The difference in basic characteristics between complex and random 
networks directly affects key properties, such as control strategy ob -
jectives, game income calculation, and decision algorithm design of 
network attack and defense games. Therefore, it is of urgent necessity 
and practical significance to study attack–defense game models and 
defense decision methods that can adapt to the characteristics of 
scale-free complex networks. 
Currently, the main complex network security defense decision 
methods include defense decision methods based on the optimal control 
theory and immune defense strategies. Considering defense decision 
methods based on optimal control, in [25], the authors proposed an 
optimal isolation strategy based on scale-free networks, introduced the 
isolation rate as a function of degree, proposed the basic reproduction 
number as a balance point, and constructed an appropriate Lyapunov 
function to prove the stability of the basic reproduction number. 
Reference [26] studied the optimal defense control problem in complex 
networks, considering the optimal control solutions of chain, star, ring, 
and scale-free networks. Reference [27] proposed an individual-based 
weight adaptation mechanism, where a defender can influence the 
spreading speed of a virus through a network by changing the individual 
contact strength. In [28], the infection process of two viruses competing 
on complex networks was studied, an optimization problem was defined 
to find an optimal control strategy in different stages of the infectious 
disease process, and an iterative algorithm was proposed to derive an 
optimal treatment strategy. In [29], an APT defense problem was 
modeled as an optimal control problem, comprehensively considered 
the influence of direct attack and lateral diffusion, and obtained the APT 
defense strategy by solving the optimal control problem. In the study of 
immune defense strategies in [30], the authors compared the effects of 
random immunity, target immunity, acquaintance immunity, and 
high-risk immunity on the propagation process for the defense of mal -
ware propagation; defense strategy was combined with software di -
versity. Reference [31] proposed an observer node model, which can 
identify infections by interacting with infected neighbors and inform 
other neighbors to get vaccinated, thereby effectively suppressing the 
infection. Reference [32] defined the degree of nodes as a continuous 
variable in directed networks, considered immune strategies such as 
optimal immunity and random immunity, combined target and ac -
quaintance immunity with several different networks, and proposed a 
computational network immune system defense method. Reference [33] 
combined the topological characteristics of a network with nodes’ ac -
tivities and used a metric method, which includes node importance and 
activity level, to estimate the importance of nodes, and then determines 
key nodes to implement defense strategies to elevate network security 
defense. 
The above-mentioned research on security defense decisions for 
complex networks focuses only on the defense side while ignoring the 
core key of network security: attack and defense confrontation. There 
has been almost no research on confrontation and interaction between 
attack and defense behaviors, which weakens the accuracy and effec -
tiveness of defense decisions. In the actual network attack–defense 
confrontation, the attack and defense behaviors have the characteristics 
of strong correlation, and the state and evolution of network security 
depend on the confrontation process and results of the attacker and 
defender. Therefore, it is necessary to research the defense decision 
methods based on a comprehensive consideration of the interactive 
relationship between attack and defense behaviors.

This article tries to solve the problem that the current defense deci -
sion methods based on game theory lack consideration of the actual 
network structure, and using a complex network defense decision 
method is insufficient for confrontational analysis of attack and defense 
behaviors. Regarding the problem of real-time defense decisions in 
scale-free complex networks, this paper proposes a network 
attack – defense differential game model and designs a defense decision 
algorithm based on solving the equilibrium strategy of the saddle point 
game. The effectiveness of the model and algorithm are verified by 
comparative experiments. 
The main contributions of this article are as follows:  
1) A propagation dynamics model of a scale-free network is established 
to perform the evolution analysis of the security state of a complex 
network with large differences in the degree distribution of nodes. 
Then, the description of network attack and defense strategies and 
method for calculating gains are designed based on the confrontation 
analysis of the network attack and defense behavior and a change in 
the security state of network nodes;  
2) On the basis of the evolution analysis of the network security state 
and differential game theory, a real-time game model of the network 
attack and defense is constructed, and a method for calculating the 
saddle point equilibrium strategy of the network attack and defense 
game is proposed; the optimal defense strategy selection is also 
realized based on the saddle point equilibrium strategy algorithm;  
3) Based on the degree of average real-world connection, a simulated 
network is constructed to conduct experiments, and the change 
process and stability of the optimal defense strategy are analyzed. 
The defense gains of the saddle point equilibrium strategy and the 
random strategy are compared, and a comparison with other typical 
complex strategies is conducted. In addition, a comparison of 
network defense decision methods is also performed to verify the 
effectiveness and performance of the proposed models and methods. 
The remaining work of this paper is arranged as follows. Section 2 
analyzes the scale-free network real-time attack and defense game. In 
Section 3 , the game equilibrium solution is solved and the optimal de -
fense strategy selection method is given. In Section 4 , the simulation 
experiment is designed and analyzed. Section 5 summarizes the full text. 
2. Analysis of scale-free network real-time attack and defense 
game 
According to the degree block approximation theory of complex 
networks [24] , the degree of network nodes is used to identify different 
nodes, assuming nodes that approximate statistically with the same 
degree; namely, these nodes have similar network attributes, behavioral 
characteristics, and decision preference. Nodes with the same degree of 
connectivity are defined as a node set and used as a basic unit to 
establish a scale-free network propagation dynamics model and analyze 
and study the process of network attack – defense behavior confrontation 
and network security state evolution.

There is a large difference between a hub node and a general node in 
a scale-free network. This difference originates from the network 
structure, which is manifested as a difference in the node connectivity 
between nodes. Take the actual Internet as an example; the average 
degree is about 6.3, but the connection degree of more than 80% of the

the hub and general nodes, the defense ideas and strategies for different 
nodes should be treated differently. Therefore, it is necessary to take the 
node degree k as a key parameter in the analysis of network security 
state evolution. 
The four states of nodes are as follows:  
1) S (susceptible state): A node can provide services normally; due to 
the lack of effective protection, a node is vulnerable and easy to be 
invaded by attackers. After being attacked and invaded, a node 
transfers from the susceptible state to the infected state;  
2) I (infected state): An attacker can use an infected node to attack a 
susceptible node and consequently spread the infection scope; an 
attacker can also further destroy an infected node and take down a 
server; a defender can repair the infected node to bring it into the 
repair state;  
3) R (repaired state): A repaired node has immune efficacy and cannot 
be re-infected by the same attack. With time, the probability of an 
attack strategy mutation increases, the immune efficacy gradually 
disappears, and a node returns to the susceptible state;  
4) D (destructed state): The node is destructed by the attack and cannot 
provide services.

3.3. Model and method comparison 
The proposed model and method were compared with related 
studies, as shown in Table 1 . The network structure type indicates the 
network structure used to model and describe the real network. 
Currently, the existing models mainly include random network and 
scale-free network structure models. The actual Internet is highly het -
erogeneous, where a few nodes occupy many connections, which is 
closer to the characteristics of a scale-free network. References [14] and 
[20] used a random network structure, which is far from a real-world 
network. In contrast, in [ 29 , 30 ], and this article, a scale-free network 
structure is used, which is more in line with the real network environ -
ment and the practicability of research work. The attack – defense 
confrontation analysis method shows which perspective and ideas are 
used to conduct the network attack – defense analysis and defense deci -
sion research, including fixed strategies, unilateral optimal control 
methods, and attack – defense game analysis. The fixed strategy [29] and 
the unilateral optimal control method [30] both assume that an attack 
adopts a fixed strategy and then optimize the defense decision for the 
fixed attack strategy. The essence of network security lies in attack and 
defense confrontation. The attack and defense behaviors in network 
confrontation are characterized by strong correlation and tight coupling. 
When the confrontation and interaction factors of attack and defense 
behaviors are not considered, that not only deviates from the actual 
situation but also affects the accuracy and efficiency of defense de -
cisions. References [ 14 , 20 ], and this article use the game model to 
analyze the network attack and defense process and then study the de -
fense decision algorithm, which is more in line with reality. Defensive

4.3. Comparative analysis of network defense effectiveness 
To verify the network defense effectiveness of the proposed method, 
comparative experiments were conducted, and the proposed method 
was compared with the optimal control method [29] and the target 
immune strategy method [30] . Similar to the experimental design in the 
previous section, 20 attack strategies were randomly selected, and 20 
sets of experiments were conducted independently. The duration of each 
set of experiments was 30 min, and the defense gains of the three 
methods were collected separately, as shown in Fig. 5 . In Fig. 5 , the 
horizontal coordinate indicates the serial number of stochastic attack 
strategies, the vertical coordinate indicates the defense improvements, 
and the horizontal line represents the average improvement under each 
strategy. 
As shown in Fig. 5 , the average gain of the proposed method was 
287.7, and the maximum gain was 292.6; the average gain of the 
optimal control method was 243.9, and the average gain of the immune 
strategy method was 268.2. For the first, second, and fifth attack stra -
tegies, the defense gain of the optimal control method was close to that 
of the proposed method. For the fourth attack strategy, the defense gain 
of the immune strategy method was closer to that of the proposed 
method. The main reason was that both the optimal control method and 
the immune strategy method assumed that the behavior of an attacker 
was fixed and then optimized the defense decision for the fixed attack 
strategy. Therefore, the optimal control and immune strategy methods 
could achieve high defense gains for specific attack strategies but could 
not maintain high defense effectiveness against all attack strategies.

5. Conclusion 
The real networks are highly heterogeneous, with a few central 
nodes having many connections and the rest of the nodes having very 
few connections. The network attack and defense behaviors are 
confrontational and dependent, and a defender must consider the 
impact of attack behaviors when making decisions. In view of that, this 
paper combines scale-free complex networks with differential games to 
develop a defense decision method by considering the network structure 
characteristics and the attack and defense confrontational behaviors. In 
this paper, differential equations of network state evolution are derived, 
and a differential game model of attack and defense is constructed and 
used to obtain the saddle point equilibrium of the attack and defense 
game and design an optimal defense strategy selection algorithm. The 
effectiveness and defense efficacy of the game model and defense deci -
sion algorithm are verified by simulation experiments. The results pre -
sented in this paper have a guiding value for the security management of 
complex networks and can provide insight into modeling methods for 
research in the fields of social network opinion management and control 
[39] , dynamic scheduling of large transportation networks [40] , and 
smart grid security control [41] . 
Future work could improve the granularity of defense decisions for 
scale-free complex networks and enhance the network defense effec -
tiveness and the accuracy of security control by introducing deep rein -
forcement learning techniques. In this way, near real-time attack and 
defense game analysis and optimal strategy selection at the large-scale 
node level could be achieved when increasing the latency of defense 
decisions moderately.
